How long does each drive take at rush hour, compared with quiet hours?

Each row is Waze's best available drive-time estimate between two fixed points, not a measured trip, and not a named road (Waze picked the road). Quiet-hour = the fastest 10% of that route's own readings. Rush hour = the busiest of the 24 clock hours for that route, Monday to Thursday only (at least 100 readings on 20 days). It is not always the morning or evening hour you would expect. Speed = each reading's own distance divided by its own minutes, then the median, because Waze changes the route and so the distance. Minutes, UAE time. Click a row for its day. Ranked by extra minutes.

Where the jam map showed slow or blocked roads

Waze live-map jam records, Dubai and the northern emirates. Last recorded jams = the last good sweep, levels 1 to 4. Recurring slow spots = places that jammed often across the whole study. Blocked or closed = level 5 segments (speed 0, about 130 m long) from the last sweep, plus cells that looked jammed at almost every hour of every day (roadworks, closures or staging areas); these are not counted as congestion.

Does Waze ever suggest a side street first?

The pair is one trip (same start and end). One leg is the route Waze chose as its first choice (the main road); the other is a named side-street alternative. A side street can only appear as a lower-ranked alternative, so this table does not show what drivers actually do. It shows what Waze suggested.
